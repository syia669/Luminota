Getting started

You're three steps
away from ready.

No setup. No accounts. No learning curve. Here's everything you need to go from purchase to planning in under five minutes.

✦

Duplicate the template into your Notion

When you open your purchase link, you'll land on the Luminota template page in Notion. Look for the Duplicate button in the top-right corner of the page — it looks like two overlapping squares. Click it, and Notion will copy the entire workspace directly into your account.

💡

You'll need a free Notion account to duplicate. If you don't have one yet, Notion will prompt you to sign up — it takes about a minute and the free plan is all you need.

✦

Start with Trip Logistics

This is where your pre-trip planning lives. Open the Trip Logistics section and work through each hub to make the template yours.

Accommodations — add your lodge bookings, confirmation numbers, check-in and check-out dates
Flights — log your flight details, confirmation codes, and arrival times
Car Rental — track your rental details, pickup location, and return info
Packing List — built specifically for Yellowstone and Grand Teton, check things off as you pack
✦

Browse your itinerary — and make it yours

The day-by-day itinerary is fully built out and ready to use as-is. Each day has its route, attractions, drive times, and lodging already mapped. You don't need to change anything — but you can.

✏️

Feel free to add your own notes, photos, and memos to any day or attraction card. Notion makes it easy — just click into any page and start typing or drag in an image.

⚠️

Important: No cell signal inside the parks

Most of Yellowstone and Grand Teton has little to no cell service. Make sure to enable offline mode in Notion before you leave — otherwise your template won't be accessible once you're inside the park.

1
Open the Notion app on your phone or tablet
2
Go to Settings → Account Settings
3
Scroll to Offline Mode and toggle it on
4
Open your template while on Wi-Fi to cache it — then it's available without signal
